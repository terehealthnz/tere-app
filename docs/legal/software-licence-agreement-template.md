# Software Licence Agreement — Template

**Status:** Draft template — first pass, **NOT execution-ready**.
Requires review by a NZ tech / health lawyer before use with any
licensee.

**Purpose:** master template for licensing the Tere Health platform
to third-party organisations (GP practices, PHOs, corporate customers).
Not for individual clinicians who contract to deliver Tere services
via the platform — those are governed by the separate Contractor
Agreement.

**Underwriter alignment:** drafted to sit within Delta Insurance's
Technology Liability policy (per Louise Kingi / Delta correspondence
2026-08-26). Sections **8 (Data Privacy and Security)**, **9 (Data
Ownership and Access)**, **10 (Support and Maintenance)**, and **15
(Limitation of Liability)** are the four clause families Delta
specifically flagged as expected. Section **12 (Clinical
Responsibility)** and Section **13 (Medical Device Status)** preserve
the "not used for diagnostic purposes" characterisation Delta
underwrote to.

*(Delta-flagged clause)*
