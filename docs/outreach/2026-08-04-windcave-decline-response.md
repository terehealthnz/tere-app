Kia ora Jebel,

Thank you for the reply, though I want to place on record my disappointment with how this process has been handled.

Over the past several weeks Tere Health invested significant time to build, integrate, and certify against your Hosted Payment Page. Your team subsequently requested additional compliance documentation, which we provided in full, and then required notarised paperwork — which we paid for at our own cost. After all of that, the outcome is a one-paragraph decline with no reasons given and no ability to respond.

I appreciate that underwriting decisions are ultimately Windcave's to make. What is harder to accept is that the decline came only after we had spent development effort and money on your specific requirements. If our application was going to fail on grounds that are not disclosable, the fair thing to do was to communicate that at the point those grounds became apparent — not after we had completed the technical integration and paid for notarisation.

The absence of any explanation compounds this. I understand you may not be "in a position to provide further details," but that framing is exactly the problem: it leaves us with no ability to correct a misunderstanding, no way to seek a different pathway with your team, and no useful signal to take to another provider.

We chose Windcave specifically because it is New Zealand-based and we wanted to support a local business. That preference no longer outweighs the cost of the process you have just put us through. Tere Health will be moving to an offshore merchant service provider, and I will not be recommending Windcave to other New Zealand health-tech founders.

I would welcome feedback if any is available, in the form of the actual reason for the decline — even after the fact — so we can avoid the same experience with our next provider.
