# Media + Communications Policy

**Version:** v1.0 · **Date:** 3 September 2026 · **Owner:** Patrick Herling
**Applies to:** all staff, contractors, providers, and anyone speaking about Tere Health in any public capacity.

---

## 1. Who speaks for Tere

- **All media enquiries** (print, broadcast, podcast, industry press) → **Patrick Herling** first, Rachel Thomas backup.
- **Clinical + safety topics** → Rachel Thomas (Medical Director) is the clinical voice; Patrick may hand over.
- **Regulator + policy topics** → Patrick Herling directly.
- **No other staff, provider, or contractor** speaks to media about Tere without written approval from Patrick or Rachel.

## 2. If media contacts you

- **Do not confirm, deny, or comment.** Say: "Thanks — I'll ask our comms lead to get back to you today." Then forward to Patrick within 1 hour.
- Do not go off-record. There is no "off-record."
- Do not provide patient-specific comment even in general terms.

## 3. Public statements about incidents

If Tere makes a public statement about a security, clinical, or reputational incident:

- Patrick + Rachel jointly draft the statement.
- Legal (MPS or external counsel) reviews before publication.
- The statement acknowledges what happened, what we're doing, what patients should do, and how to contact us.
- No blame apportionment to individuals until formal investigation completes.

## 4. Social media

### 4.1 Tere corporate accounts
- Only Patrick or a delegate (documented) posts on official Tere accounts (LinkedIn, X, Instagram if activated).
- Nothing that identifies a specific patient without explicit written consent.
- No clinical advice via social — always redirect to a consultation.
- No comment on active investigations, complaints, or regulator matters.

### 4.2 Staff personal accounts
- Staff can identify Tere as their employer but must include the standard disclaimer where they discuss health topics: "views are my own, not clinical advice, not Tere Health's position."
- Staff must not post about identifiable patients or specific consults, even in general terms.
- Staff must not post patient photographs, test results, or clinical details.
- If a staff member sees another staff member breaching this, they raise it via the whistleblowing policy.

## 5. Marketing communications

- All patient-facing marketing (website, ads, email, print) reviewed by Rachel for clinical accuracy before publication.
- Testimonials require written signed consent from the patient. Anonymous testimonials must be flagged as such.
- No comparative claims about other providers ("better than Dr X") without factual substantiation.
- No claims of clinical outcomes we don't measure.
- All AI features described accurately (AI is advisory to clinicians, not decision-making).

## 6. Research + academic communications

- Any research publication naming Tere requires Rachel + Patrick joint approval.
- Conflicts of interest disclosed per journal requirements.
- Pre-prints reviewed like published papers.

## 7. Conference + industry speaking

- Individual providers may speak at conferences on general clinical topics; content about Tere specifically needs approval.
- Slides referring to Tere reviewed by Patrick.

## 8. Patient stories

- Consent is written, specific, and revocable.
- The story is anonymised unless the patient explicitly consents to identification.
- Stories reviewed for privacy (nothing that lets a stranger identify the patient) before publication.
- Patient can withdraw consent at any time; we take the story down promptly (within 5 working days).

## 9. Language + tone standards

- Plain English; avoid jargon in patient-facing content.
- Culturally appropriate for Aotearoa audience; Te Reo Māori welcomed where authentic.
- Respectful of disabled, elderly, mental-health-affected communities.
- Fact-checked (especially clinical claims).

## 10. If something goes wrong

- Publish a correction promptly.
- Retain a change log on formal documents.
- If a public statement was misleading, issue a plain correction, not a spin.

## 11. Records

- Every media interaction logged in `docs/comms/YYYY-MM-DD-source.md`.
- Every social-media post about Tere by staff captured (screenshot + archive).
- Every marketing communication archived with approval trail.

## 12. Review

- Reviewed annually or after any material incident.
