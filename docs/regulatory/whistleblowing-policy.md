# Whistleblowing Policy — Tere Health Ltd

**Statute:** Protected Disclosures (Protection of Whistleblowers) Act 2022.
**Version:** v1.0 (draft) · **Date:** 3 September 2026
**Owner:** Patrick Herling (CMO). Rachel Thomas backup.

This policy sets out how anyone connected with Tere Health — employee, contractor, provider, admin, patient, or member of the public — can raise a concern about serious wrongdoing without fear of retaliation.

---

## 1. Who this policy protects

**Everyone**, including:
- Employees + contractors of Tere Health
- Providers (medical, nursing, allied)
- Admin staff
- Patients + their families
- External vendors + partners
- Members of the public

## 2. What counts as a protected disclosure

Under the Protected Disclosures Act 2022 s10, a protected disclosure is information about **serious wrongdoing** that a person reasonably believes is true. Serious wrongdoing includes:

- **Unlawful conduct** — anything illegal
- **Serious risk to public health or safety** — e.g. we're systematically failing to recognise a red flag
- **Serious risk to the environment** — n/a for us
- **Gross mismanagement of public funds** — n/a (we're private)
- **Serious risk to the maintenance of law** — including corruption
- **Oppressive, improperly discriminatory, or grossly negligent conduct** by a public sector organisation — includes hospitals we interact with
- **Unlawful or corrupt use of public sector funds or resources**

For Tere specifically, the most relevant categories are:
- **Clinical safety** — a colleague is prescribing dangerously, or the platform has a defect that risks patients
- **Privacy + security** — someone is misusing patient data
- **Fraud** — falsified ACC billing, misuse of company funds
- **Discrimination** — a colleague is treating patients or staff unequally
- **Regulatory breach** — we're not complying with MCNZ, HDC, HNZ, Medsafe, ACC, Privacy Act obligations
- **Bullying or harassment** — including sexual harassment

## 5. Our commitments

If you raise a protected disclosure:

1. **We will investigate** within 20 working days of receipt (extendable with reason).
2. **We will keep your identity confidential** unless you consent to disclosure, or law requires it, or it becomes essential to a fair investigation and you've been told.
3. **We will NOT retaliate** — no dismissal, demotion, discipline, exclusion, or unfavourable treatment because you raised a concern in good faith. Retaliation is a criminal offence under the Act and is grounds for immediate dismissal at Tere.
4. **We will keep you updated** on progress and outcome, so far as confidentiality permits.
5. **We will act on findings** — if wrongdoing is confirmed, we will fix the underlying issue and, where warranted, notify the appropriate external body.

## 6. What if the disclosure is malicious or made in bad faith

- Deliberately false disclosures are NOT protected.
- We will investigate the disclosure on its merits first; only if it's clearly malicious will we consider disciplinary action against the discloser.
- Being wrong in good faith is fully protected. Uncertainty is fully protected.

## 7. Records
