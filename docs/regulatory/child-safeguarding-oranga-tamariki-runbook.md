# Child Safeguarding — Oranga Tamariki mandatory reporting runbook

**Owner:** Clinical lead + Chief Medical Officer
**Applies:** Every Tere Health provider seeing a patient of any age
**Trigger:** Any safeguarding concern raised, disclosed, or observed during a consult

## 1. Legal + regulatory context

- **Oranga Tamariki Act 1989** — every practitioner working with children has a duty to report suspected abuse or neglect to Oranga Tamariki or the Police.
- **HDC Code of Rights** — Right 4 (services of appropriate standard), Right 6 (information), Right 7 (informed consent) all bear on how safeguarding concerns are handled.
- **MCNZ / NCNZ** — expect documented recognition of safeguarding responsibilities as a scope-of-practice standard.
- **Family Violence Act 2018** — reporting mandate + risk assessment obligations if family violence disclosed.

**Reporting is NOT dependent on proof.** Reasonable suspicion is the threshold.

## 2. When to flag "safeguarding concern" in the Tere app

Use the panel on ClinicianPatient (`⚠ Raise safeguarding concern`) whenever any of the following occurs during a consult:

- **Direct disclosure** — child, dependent adult, or bystander says they are being harmed.
- **Indirect disclosure** — comments consistent with abuse ("Daddy hits Mummy", "I'm not allowed to eat until…").
- **Clinical suspicion** — injuries inconsistent with history, unexplained bruising/burns, developmental concerns, malnutrition.
- **Observed environment** — unsafe/unsanitary home visible on camera, other unsafe adults present.
- **Threats to self/others** made during a consult where a child or dependent is present or affected.
- **Coercion** — clinician's judgement that the patient (any age) is unable to speak freely because someone is controlling the interaction.

The panel captures: **type, notes (min 20 chars — factual, direct quotes preferred, non-judgemental), timestamp, provider identity.** This becomes evidence.

## 3. Immediate actions (during / immediately after the consult)

1. **If a child is in immediate danger:** call **111** while the consult is still open. Do not end the video until police / ambulance are dispatched.
2. **If disclosure is fresh:**
   - Acknowledge the person for telling you. Do not interrogate or lead.
   - Ensure they can leave / disengage safely.
   - Provide safety information — for adults: `1737` (24/7 free call/text), `Women's Refuge 0800 REFUGE (0800 733 843)`, `Shine 0508 744 633`.
3. **Do NOT** promise confidentiality — you are required to report.
4. **Do NOT** confront the alleged perpetrator or attempt to remove the child yourself.
5. **DO** flag in Tere (panel button) immediately — this stops the concern getting lost between end-of-consult and reporting.

## 4. Oranga Tamariki notification — how

**Non-immediate (working hours + no immediate danger):**
- Complete the online report: **[https://www.orangatamariki.govt.nz/report-a-concern](https://www.orangatamariki.govt.nz/report-a-concern)**
- Include: your name + MCNZ/NCNZ number, contact, patient identifying details (name, DOB, NHI, address, guardian), nature of concern, evidence, immediate risks.

**Immediate / after-hours / uncertain:**
- Call the **National Contact Centre: 0508 FAMILY (0508 326 459)** — 24/7.
- Ask for a **Report of Concern**. Take the reference number.

**If Police involvement warranted (immediate danger, criminal act witnessed):**
- Call **111**. Report to Oranga Tamariki afterwards.

**Family violence to adult(s) with children in the home:**
- Report BOTH the family violence (via 111 if immediate, or Family Harm Referral) AND the child concern to Oranga Tamariki.

## 5. Documentation obligations

Every safeguarding-flagged consult must have:

- The Tere panel entry (auto-captured on flag)
- A separate clinical note describing what was observed / disclosed / done (verbatim quotes)
- Time + date of the Oranga Tamariki / Police call, name of the person spoken to, reference number
- Any advice received and actions taken

All of this goes into the consultation record — it will be visible to the CMO on audit and forms your defence if practice is later questioned.

## 6. What to tell the family

- **If safe to do so:** briefly explain that you are required by law to notify Oranga Tamariki about certain concerns; you cannot keep this confidential.
- **Do NOT tell an alleged perpetrator** what will be reported.
- **Do NOT specify what will happen next** — that is Oranga Tamariki's role.
- If the family becomes agitated or leaves the consult, this is itself information — record it.

## 7. Escalation to Tere leadership

Every safeguarding flag auto-alerts the on-call admin + CMO (via audit_log routing).

CMO reviews within 24 hours. Panel entry + Oranga Tamariki reference number logged in Admin > Compliance.

Every safeguarding case is a standing agenda item at the next Clinical Governance Meeting for peer review (not adversarial — for shared learning).

## 8. Ongoing support for the provider

Safeguarding disclosures are cumulatively traumatic. After ANY safeguarding flag:

- CMO checks in within 48 hours.
- Access to **MPS peer support** (MPS members) or **Vitae EAP** if enrolled.
- Consider yourself on the peer-review roster for the next CGM cycle; case discussed with names anonymised.
